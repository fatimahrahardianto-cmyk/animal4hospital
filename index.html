📖 Anomaly Hospital — Doctor's Survival & Field Guide

1. Check-In, 3D ID Card, & Treatment Flow

  • Step 1 (Snapshot & CCTV): Take a Polaroid photo with the g-cam [C] and check the CCTV PC [E].
  • Step 2 (Decline or Print): If it's an anomaly, press the Red Shutters Button to decline them, or shoot/tase them with [T]. If normal, click Confirm & Print Tag on the PC.
  • Step 3 (3D ID Card): Walk to the Printer [E] to pick up the 3D rectangular blue/gray PATIENT ID Card, then press [E] on a Ward Bed (Rooms 101–108) to assign the patient.
  • Step 4 (10 Hallway Medicines): Collect the patient's 1–3 required items from the Hallway Shelves (Pill Bottle, Herbs, Cough Syrup, Maple Syrup, Medkit, Thermometer, IV Drops, Eye Drops, Ointment, Bandages) and press [E] at their bed. After a 5s recovery timer, normal patients stand up, walk out of the room, and disappear cleanly; disguised anomalies turn glowing RED, say "I'm hungry!", and hunt you!

2. Anomaly Probabilities & Special Entities

  • 65% Normal Patients: Safe pastel palette (never red or black, except Rathew the black rat).
  • 15% CCTV Anomalies: Only visible on the CCTV monitor.
  • 10% Camera Photo Anomalies: 5% have subtle ear/shape differences in the Polaroid; 5% show horrific wide red eyes that drain sanity.
  • 5% UV Anomalies: Only visible with Liz's UV Camera [U] right in front of their face.
  • "Don't Look Up" Ceiling Monster: Keep your camera pointed straight/down or calm it with Eye Drops.
  • Under-Bed Monster: Avoid red bed zones, struggle with [E], or feed it Maple Syrup, Organ, or Transplant.
  • Slimebert (Shift 10+): Immune to guns/tasers! Stun with Fire Extinguisher or melt with Cough Syrup.
  • Death Ritual (Shift 5 & 11): Extinguish all candles in 35s using Eye Drops (0 sanity loss), [E], or Taser the patient!
